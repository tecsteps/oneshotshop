# Discounts and Promotions

All amounts are net. Dates are inclusive and use the shop's time zone (see `business-rules.md`).

## General Rules

- **Goods value** means the merchandise net total after customer-group discounts and before promotions. It excludes deposits, shipping and VAT. Minimum order values are always checked against the goods value.
- At most **one promotion code** can be used per order. Code entry is not case-sensitive.
- **Automatic promotions** apply without a code and can be combined with one promotion code. Automatic promotions are applied first. A percentage code is then calculated on the eligible amount that remains after them.
- Products that are excluded from discounts in the catalog are never eligible. The same applies to lines with a customer-negotiated price.
- Deposits are never discounted.
- A promotion never reduces a line or an order below €0.00. A fixed discount is capped at the eligible amount.
- A code that is expired, not yet valid, deactivated, used up, or not allowed for the customer is rejected with a clear message. An applied code that stops qualifying when the cart changes (for example because the goods value drops below the minimum) is removed, and the customer is told why.
- A use of a code counts when the order is placed. If that order is cancelled, the use is given back.
- "Active period" 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 means the promotion is valid on every day in that range.

## Automatic Promotions

### Beer Crate Bonus — Buy 6, Pay 5

- Applies to BEER-001 (Pilsner, 0.5 l crate).
- For every 6 crates of BEER-001 in the cart, 1 crate is free. The free crate is valued at the crate price that applies to that line. For example, 12 crates means 2 free crates, and 11 crates means 1 free crate.
- Deposits are still charged for every crate, including free ones.
- Only single crates count. Crates on pallets of BEER-001 do not count toward the bonus and are never free.
- Active period: 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31.

### Prosecco Carton Deal

- 10 % off WINE-005 cartons when the cart contains at least 2 cartons of WINE-005.
- Applies only to the carton packaging unit. Single bottles do not count toward the 2 cartons and are not discounted.
- Active period: 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31.

### Free Balsamic Vinegar from €500

- An order with a goods value of €500.00 or more receives one bottle of OIL-004 (Balsamic Vinegar of Modena, 500 ml) free of charge as an extra line at €0.00.
- Limited to one free bottle per order, and only while OIL-004 is in stock.
- Active period: 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31.
