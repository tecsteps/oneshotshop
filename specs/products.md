# Product Catalog

Spreegrund Food Service is a Berlin-based wholesaler supplying restaurants, cafés, hotels, caterers and independent retailers with food, beverages and kitchen supplies.

All prices in this catalog are **net prices in euro, excluding VAT and excluding deposits**. Deposits are listed separately for each product and are charged on top of the price.

Each product names its image file; the path is relative to this document, so the images live in the `images/` folder next to it. Packaging units have their own images. Images of packaging units show the exact number of items wherever it is countable; pictures of pallets, loose goods sold by weight, multi-layer boxes and nested items are illustrations, and the text defines the content.

Some products are also sold by the **pallet**, a packing level above the crate. Pallets are bulky items, and each Euro pallet carries its own deposit.

Stock is kept per warehouse (see `shipping.md`). Unless a product states a split, all of its stock is in WH-BER. Products sourced on demand or shipped directly by a supplier are not stocked.

Stock is always stated in the smallest unit in which the product is counted (its base unit), for example bottles, cans, pieces, kilograms or litres. Ordering a larger packaging unit consumes the corresponding number of base units from stock.

Unless a product says otherwise:

- it can be ordered in every packaging unit listed for it, in whole numbers of that unit,
- it is stored at ambient temperature,
- it is eligible for customer-group discounts and promotions,
- it cannot be ordered beyond its current stock,
- it is shown as low stock when 10 or fewer base units are available (staff can change this threshold per product).

Some products come in **variants**, for example sizes. A product with variants appears only once in the catalog and has one product page, on which the customer chooses the variant. Each variant has its own SKU, price and stock, and the cart, order and invoice show the chosen variant. The product itself cannot be ordered without choosing a variant.

Some products name **related products**, for example the same drink in another flavour or bottle size. Related products are separate products with their own product pages and their own catalog entries. A product page links to its related products, and the relation always goes both ways.

Some products offer **options**, for example extra toppings. Options are chosen on the product page and change the product's price, but they are neither variants nor separate products: they have no SKU and no stock of their own.

Some products name **accessories**: products that fit with them, for example lids for to-go cups. The product page suggests its accessories and lets the customer add them to the cart directly from there. Unlike related products, accessories are add-ons rather than alternatives, and the suggestion goes one way only.

Some products belong to a **restricted assortment**: they are visible and orderable only for certain customer groups or customers. For everyone else, guests included, such a product does not exist in the shop: it does not appear in categories, search results, related products, accessories, bundles, quick order or order lists, and its product page cannot be opened. All products in the category Beverages › Spirits are restricted to the customer groups Gastronomy and Key Account. A bundle that contains a restricted product has the same restriction.

Some products are **bundles**: a fixed set of other products sold together under their own SKU, with their own product page and catalog entry, at a fixed price. A bundle has no stock of its own; how many bundles can be ordered follows from the stock of its contents.
